Built on Project CETI · WhAM (arXiv:2512.02206, NeurIPS 2025). Coda notation follows the standard click-count + rhythm-class convention. Click-consonant descriptions follow the Ladefoged & Traill account of ǃXóõ.

Human clicks are velaric ingressive consonants; sperm whale clicks pattern as vowel-like carriers. The word “click” is a false cognate across the two domains — a null result in this comparison is the expected result, not a failure.
